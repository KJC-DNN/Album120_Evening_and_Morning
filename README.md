# Album 120 — Evening and Morning
Very Good, Then Rest

Creation Days 3–6, humanity in God’s image, the garden of Eden, restored access to the tree of life, the Body of Christ as temple, and the seventh-day rest.

5. According to Their Kinds (Teaching)
6. In Our Image (Day 6 – Narrative
7. The Garden Planted Eastward (Eden – Spoken Story)
8. The Way Guarded, the Way Restored (Tree of Life – Promising)
9. You Are God’s Temple (Body of Christ – Teaching – 1 Cor 3:16; 6:19)
10. Living Stones, Holy Temple (Ephesians 2 / 1 Peter 2)
11. And He Rested (Day 7 – Praise)
12. ஏழாம் நாள் இளைப்பாறுதல்
    
# Study note: 
Days 3–6 fill the formed world; humanity bears God’s image; the Spirit now makes believers and the Church His temple; the seventh day crowns the work with rest.

# Prayer note: 
Creator and Redeemer, You made all things very good and You open the way back to life. Teach us to rest in You.
